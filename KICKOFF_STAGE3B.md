**A. Objective × coupling (the corrected Section 6.5).** Same settings for all four: `--nc 32 --nf 64 --steps 1500 --base 24 --batch 2 --rms-delta 2.0 --device mps`.
1. `--out runs/A_flow_phys` (default: flow, physical coupling)
2. `--regression --out runs/A_reg_phys`
3. `--coupling independent --out runs/A_flow_indep`
4. `--coupling independent --regression --out runs/A_reg_indep`
For each, record in the octave band: emulator-mode `r` and `P/P_true`, and `r^2`; generative-mode `P/P_true` and `r`. Then plot the four models in the `(r, P/P_true)` plane together with the two lines `P/P = r^2` and `P/P = 1` (one small matplotlib figure, `runs/A_r2_plane.png`). Expected by theory and by the cloud reference runs at 16->32 (600 CPU steps, cube window, in `reference_results/`): (2) with physical coupling should be at least as good as (1) in emulator mode, not on the `r^2` line — the reference gives regression `r=0.996, P/P=0.990` versus flow (8 Heun) `r=0.993, P/P=0.992` and flow one-step `0.972` — and its generative-mode `P/P` should still be ~1 (reference `1.03`) because a good deterministic map of a fresh octave has the right distribution. (4) should collapse towards the conditional mean given the coarse field: small `r`, `P/P` close to `r^2`. (3) keeps `P/P ≈ 1` in generative mode; in emulator mode it does *not* ignore the octave — the flow transports whatever `x_0` contains, like the rescaling in the 1D Gaussian example of the notes — so `r` stays near or above the linear baseline (reference `0.973` vs baseline `0.915`) but below the physical coupling (`0.993`), and its one-step evaluation collapses (reference `r=0.38, P/P=0.05`). Report which of these hold and, in one sentence each, what deviates. Do not describe any difference between (1) and (2) as "variance restored"; describe it as a fit/optimisation difference between the two parameterisations, and say plainly if regression wins on this smooth 2LPT toy (the case for the flow is the reduced-information / multi-stream regime of real data, not this one).

**B. Cube versus sphere window.** `--window sphere --alpha 1.0` for (1) and (2) of A, into `runs/B_flow_sphere` and `runs/B_reg_sphere`. Compare with the cube runs: octave-band `r`, `P/P_true`, and especially the generative-mode coarse-band `eps_rel` (theory: the sphere re-samples the coarse corners that the retained field is informative about, so generative-mode consistency should be worse). **D. How much of the detail is deterministic given the coarse ICs (review point 1).** Run

The number that matters is the real-data one.
